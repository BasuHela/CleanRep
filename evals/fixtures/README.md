Generated by `scripts.gen_landmarks.generate_range_eval` (also called by `make eval`), seeds 65100–65104, landmark noise σ=0.002 and 5% dropped frames. These are constructed side-view landmarks, **not recordings or device-accuracy evidence**.

The manifest runs each fixture through squat, box_squat, counterbalance_squat, tempo_squat and squat_jump (25 cases). Each attempt has an independent counted/uncounted label and time window, so a false positive cannot shift subsequent fault or abstention labels. Jumps remain unrated; tempo/counterbalance retain their targeted fault lists. E-REP-1 reports these separately from the original 200-set sample and requires all 25 to match exactly.
